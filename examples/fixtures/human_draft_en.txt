I deleted the staging database twice this month. Both times it was a Friday.

The first time, the migration script had a default that pointed at prod. Nobody caught it in review because the diff was 400 lines and the reviewer was on a train.

The second time I was moving fast and ran the wrong tab.

We now have three things we didn't have before: every migration prints the target host and asks for confirmation, staging burns get restored from a nightly snapshot that we actually tested, and the deploy checklist has one line that says "read the host out loud."

That last one sounds stupid. It's also the one that caught a mistake two weeks later.

I'm not sure any of this generalizes. We're a team of nine and our staging data is disposable. If your staging data matters, the confirmation prompt won't save you.
